# Answer key: CV and LinkedIn final exam (instructor only, not shown to students)

## Part 1: CV rewrite (messy CV for Tunde)
What strong work looks like:
- Summary: names the role (customer support), a strength with proof, remote role. No buzzwords.
- Six bullets: action verb + what + result, tailored to chat, email, records, calm with upset customers, spreadsheets. E.g. "Resolved 25+ customer enquiries daily in person and by WhatsApp, keeping replies under 10 minutes (example)", "Cut stock-count errors by 30% with a weekly checklist in Excel (example)", "Organised 4 youth events for 100+ attendees, managing registration in a spreadsheet (example)".
- Skills: job keywords used honestly (written English, chat and email support, accurate records, Excel/Google Sheets, customer care).
- Removals to expect: the objective (replaced by a summary), age/marital status/religion, hobbies unrelated to the job, unprofessional email (tundeboss99 -> a professional address), vague duties ("Responsible for customers", "Did stock", "Helped manager"), generic skills ("hard working", "team player").
- Honesty: invented details must be marked "(example)".

## Part 2: LinkedIn
Headline with keywords (e.g. "Customer support agent | Chat and email | Excel | Remote work seeker"). About in 3 to 5 short lines. Three bullets matching the CV. Ten skills. Featured item (portfolio, certificate).

## Part 3: note
80 to 120 words. Names role and company, two proof points matching the requirements, polite next step, no begging, no generic opener.

## Part 4: interview
STAR stories must separate S, T, A, R, focus on the candidate's own action, and end with a result.

## Part 5: scams
1 Scam (fee, WhatsApp login details, guaranteed daily pay, no application).
2 Looks real, but verify the company and link ("Check first" is also acceptable).
3 Scam (PIN request).

Rubric (suggested): CV 30, LinkedIn 15, note 10, interview 20, tracker and scams 15, reflection and honesty 10.
