# Answer key: Customer Support final exam (instructor only, not shown to students)

## Part 1: replies
Look for: greeting by name where known, acknowledgement of the feeling, a specific next step with a time, no blame, no request for PINs/passwords/others' data, correct policy (ticket 5: say no kindly, offer an alternative), warm close (ticket 6).
Ticket 4 must refuse to share another person's details and explain how the transfer can be recalled/reported through the right team.

## Part 2: triage (priority / team)
7  Urgent / Account (security: transfers not made by the customer)
8  High (or Urgent if they cannot use the service at all; accept either with reasoning) / Technical
9  Normal / Account
10 Urgent / Billing (money lost)
11 Low / Feedback
12 High / Account (cannot log in; Urgent acceptable if they have a payment due)
13 High / Billing (transfer pending 3 days)
14 Low / Technical (or Feedback)
15 Normal / Billing
16 Urgent / Account (security: OTP phishing attempt; should also advise never to share the OTP)

## Part 3: tools
Macros: personalised placeholders, empathy, specific next step and time. Article: numbered steps, mentions contacting the bank/support promptly and not sharing PINs. Escalation note must include who, what happened, what was tried, what is needed.

## Part 4: quality review
Reply X: low on all five (about 1 to 2): no empathy, no specifics, curt, no ownership.
Reply Y: high (about 4 to 5): empathy, ownership, a clear time, a polite close.
The rewrite should improve X in the style of Y.

## Part 5
20: do not tell customers to wait without information; give honest updates and escalate.
21: stay calm, empathise, offer to fix, follow the escalation policy, do not argue.
22: refuse; verify identity of the account owner only; privacy policy.
