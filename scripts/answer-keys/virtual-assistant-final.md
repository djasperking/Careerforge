## Part 2: scheduling (winter offsets: Lagos +1, London 0, New York -5, Nairobi +3, Dubai +4)
1 London 3pm Monday = 4pm Lagos. Free (team meeting is 10 to 11). OK.
2 New York 9am Tuesday = 3pm Lagos. Supplier call ends at 3pm: back to back. Propose 3:15pm or 3:30pm Lagos (10:15 / 10:30 New York) or another time; mention the buffer.
3 Nairobi 2pm Wednesday = 12pm Lagos. Conflicts with the blocked lunch (12 to 1). Propose 1pm Lagos (3pm Nairobi) or 11am Lagos (1pm Nairobi).
4 Dubai 6pm Thursday = 3pm Lagos. Free. OK.
5 London 5pm Friday = 6pm Lagos. Outside working hours (ends 5pm). Propose an earlier time, such as 3pm London = 4pm Lagos, or ask Chioma if she can stay.
6 Monday 10:30am Lagos conflicts with the 10 to 11 team meeting. Propose 11:30am or the afternoon.
Each message must show both time zones.

## Part 3: task board
Sensible owners/dates. Pay the fabric supplier (Do now, due Friday), domain renewal (Do now, high), courier delay (check today), sales sheet to accountant (due soon), enquiries (daily), price list (schedule), Instagram plan (schedule). Report: Done / Waiting / Next.

## Part 4: research
Recommendation: QuickBill free plan to start for one person; mention the invoice limit. Must flag what could not be verified (e.g. real limits, current pricing). One page, plain, recommendation clear.

## Part 6
PIN request: refuse politely, never use a client's PIN, suggest a safe route (client pays or sets up business banking access), and do not agree to secrecy from the accountant.
Deposit request: a scam; you never pay to get work; decline and stop contact.

Score: Part 2 conversions are the key check (4pm, 3pm, 12pm, 3pm, 6pm). Part 1 item 8 (phishing) is the key judgment check.
