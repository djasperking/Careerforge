## 🎓 Your final exam & what's next

You've done the hard part. You learned HTML, CSS, Flexbox, responsive design and hover effects — and you built real pages. Now let's make it official.

### The final exam

You'll find the exam under **Exams** (the **Learning** tab in your dashboard menu), or go straight to it:

👉 [Open your exams](/dashboard/exams)

**How it works**

- 🧠 **15 questions**, drawn from a larger pool — so your questions may differ from a friend's
- ⏱️ **30 minutes**
- ✅ You need **70%** to pass
- 🔁 **2 attempts**
- 🔀 Questions and answer options are shuffled

> 🧘 **Don't stress.** Everything on the exam was taught in this course. Revisit the lessons and your **cheat sheet** first, and you'll be fine.

### Before you start, quickly revise

- The page skeleton and what goes in `<head>` vs `<body>`
- Headings, links, images, lists and forms
- Semantic tags (`<header>`, `<nav>`, `<main>`, `<footer>`)
- Selectors: element, class, ID
- The box model: margin, border, padding, content
- Flexbox basics and `@media` queries

### Your certificate 🏅

- Finish **every lesson** in the course → you earn the **course completion certificate**.
- **Pass the final exam** → you earn a separate **exam certificate**.

Both have a public verification page, so you can add them to your CV and LinkedIn and employers can confirm they're real.

### What to learn next

1. **JavaScript** — make your pages interactive (buttons that do things, forms that check themselves).
2. **Build more projects** — a recipe page, a business site for a friend, a clone of a page you love. Projects are the fastest teacher.
3. **Git & GitHub** — save your work and show it publicly.
4. **Put your site online** — free hosting makes this easy.

### Use your new skills

- Add your **portfolio page** link to your Career Forge CV.
- Browse the **Jobs** board for roles that value web skills.
- Offer to build a simple page for a local business — your first client could be one conversation away.
