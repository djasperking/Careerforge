## 🏁 FINAL EXAM: Build your own website

[[workspace checks=html,css]]

No multiple-choice questions here. Your final exam is the real thing: **build a complete little website** using everything you've learned — HTML structure, text, links, images, lists, forms, CSS colours, fonts, the box model, Flexbox, hover effects and responsive design.

It's **free**, there's **no timer**, and you can't break anything. Take your time.

### Pick a topic

- 🧑‍💻 A **personal portfolio**
- 🍰 A **small business** — bakery, barber, tutor, photographer…
- 🎓 A **course or event** you'd like to promote
- ❤️ A **cause** you care about

### What your website must have

**HTML — the structure**

1. ✅ A proper skeleton: `<!DOCTYPE html>`, `<html lang="en">`, `<title>`, and the **viewport** tag
2. ✅ A `<header>` with your name or logo, and a `<nav>` with at least 3 links
3. ✅ A big `<h1>`, `<h2>` headings for each section, and real paragraphs
4. ✅ A `<main>` with at least two `<section>`s
5. ✅ A **list**, a **link**, and an **image** with `alt` text
6. ✅ A **contact form** with labels, inputs and a button
7. ✅ A `<footer>`

**CSS — the style**

8. ✅ A colour scheme and a `font-family` with a fallback
9. ✅ Padding and margin used with care
10. ✅ **Cards** with `border-radius` or `box-shadow`
11. ✅ **Flexbox** for the nav and a row of cards
12. ✅ A **hover effect** with a **transition**
13. ✅ A **media query** so it looks good on a phone

The **requirements checker** under the editor ticks each item as you build. Aim for all green — then make it look beautiful.

### 🌟 Stretch goals

- A centred container with `max-width` and `margin: 0 auto`
- A gradient background
- A testimonials or pricing section

### How you'll be graded

Your instructor opens your live website and reads your code: **requirements met**, **tidy code**, **good design**, **works on a phone**. You'll get written feedback and can resubmit to improve.
