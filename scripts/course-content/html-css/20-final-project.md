## 🚀 Final project: your personal landing page

This is it — the project that shows everything you've learned. You'll build a polished, **responsive landing page** for yourself, a small business, or an idea you care about.

### Choose your topic

Pick one (or invent your own):

- 🧑‍💻 **Personal portfolio** — who you are and what you can do
- 🍰 **A small business** — a bakery, tailor, barber, tutor, photographer…
- 🎓 **A course or event** you'd like to promote
- ❤️ **A cause** you believe in

### Requirements

Your page (one file, `index.html`, with its CSS) **must** include:

**Structure (HTML)**
1. ✅ The page skeleton with a clear `<title>` and the viewport meta tag
2. ✅ A `<header>` containing a logo/name and a `<nav>` with at least 3 links
3. ✅ A **hero section**: a big `<h1>`, one sentence of text, and a call-to-action button (`<a>` styled as a button)
4. ✅ A **features section** with **three cards** (each with a heading and text)
5. ✅ A **contact form** (name, email, message, button)
6. ✅ A `<footer>`
7. ✅ At least one **image** with proper `alt` text

**Style (CSS)**
8. ✅ A consistent colour scheme and font choice
9. ✅ The nav and the three cards laid out with **Flexbox**
10. ✅ Cards with padding, rounded corners and a shadow
11. ✅ A **hover effect with a transition** on your button and cards
12. ✅ **Responsive**: a `@media` query that stacks the cards on small screens

### 🌟 Stretch goals

- A fixed-width centred container using `max-width` and `margin: 0 auto`
- A gradient background (`linear-gradient`)
- A "testimonials" or "pricing" section

### How you'll be reviewed

- **Requirements met** (the checklist above)
- **Code quality** — tidy indentation, meaningful class names, no leftover mess
- **Design** — consistent, readable, and pleasant to look at
- **Responsiveness** — works on a phone-sized screen

### How to submit

Paste **your complete code** (HTML and CSS) in the box below. Test it on your phone first!

When this is reviewed and you've passed the final exam, you'll have something real to show employers and clients.

You've got this. 🌟
