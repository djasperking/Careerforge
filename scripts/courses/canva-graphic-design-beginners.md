## 🖥️ The Canva editor

### Tour of the editor

- **Canvas:** the page you design on.
- **Left panel:** Templates, Elements, Text, Uploads and more.
- **Top toolbar:** changes for whatever you have selected (colour, font, size).
- **Pages:** at the bottom, for multi-page designs.
- **Share / Download:** top right.

> 📌 **Remember:** click an item first. The top toolbar then shows what you can change.

::: check Where do you change an object's colour?
- The bottom bar
+ The top toolbar, after selecting the object
- Nowhere
= The toolbar is context-sensitive.
:::

### Essential moves

- **Drag** to move, **corner handles** to resize (hold Shift to keep proportions).
- **Ctrl + Z** to undo, **Ctrl + D** to duplicate.
- **Alt/Option + drag** to copy an object.
- **Position → Align** to centre things exactly.
- **Group** (Ctrl + G) to move several items together.

### Start from a template, then make it yours

Templates help beginners, but **always change colours, fonts and text** so your work does not look like everyone else's.

::: check Why change a template's colours and fonts?
+ So your design looks unique and matches the brand
- Because it is illegal to use as-is
- Because templates are broken
= Make it your own and match the client's brand.
:::

## 🌈 Colour that works

### Keep a small palette

Use **2 to 3 main colours** plus neutrals (white, black, grey). More colours usually look messy.

- **Main colour:** the brand colour, used for key items.
- **Accent colour:** a contrasting colour for buttons and highlights.
- **Neutral:** background and text.

> 📌 **Remember:** a palette of 3 colours is usually enough.

::: check How many main colours should most designs use?
- As many as possible
+ Two or three, plus neutrals
- Ten
= A small palette looks professional.
:::

### Colour has meaning

- **Blue:** trust and calm. **Green:** growth, health, money. **Red:** urgency, energy. **Yellow/orange:** friendly, optimistic. **Black/white:** modern and premium.

### Contrast: can people read it?

Text must **stand out** from its background. Dark text on a light background, or light text on a dark one, works. Light grey on white is hard to read.

> ⚠️ **Watch out:** if you have to squint, the contrast is too low.

::: check Which is easiest to read?
- Light grey text on white
+ Dark text on a light background
- Yellow text on white
= High contrast makes reading easy.
:::

### Get a palette

In Canva, open **Styles → Colour palettes**, or use a free palette generator. Pick colours from a **logo or photo** so everything matches.

## 🔤 Fonts and text

### Use two fonts, maximum

Pair **one heading font** with **one body font**. Examples: a bold sans-serif heading with a clean, simple body font.

- **Serif** (with small feet): classic, formal.
- **Sans-serif** (clean): modern, easy on screens.
- **Script** (handwriting): decorative, use only for a few words, never long text.

> 📌 **Remember:** two fonts, one hierarchy: big and bold for the main message, smaller for details.

::: check How many fonts should most designs use?
- Five or more
+ Two at most
- One per word
= Fewer fonts look more professional.
:::

### Hierarchy

Make the **most important thing the biggest**: the headline. Then supporting text, then small details like the address.

- Headline: large and bold
- Subtitle: medium
- Details: smaller, but still readable (not tiny)

### Text rules

- Keep **lines short**, with room around the text.
- **Left-align** long text. Centre is for short bits like titles.
- Avoid ALL CAPS for paragraphs.
- Use **line spacing** so text is not squashed.

::: check What should be the biggest text in a poster?
+ The headline or main message
- The phone number
- The small print
= Hierarchy guides the eye.
:::

## 📐 Layout, spacing and alignment

### White space is your friend

**Empty space** makes a design feel clean. Crowded designs feel cheap and are hard to read.

> 📌 **Remember:** if it feels crowded, remove something or make it smaller.

::: check What does white space do?
+ Makes designs cleaner and easier to read
- Wastes paper
- Hides mistakes
= Space gives elements room to breathe.
:::

### The four principles

1. **Alignment:** line things up on the same edge or centre.
2. **Proximity:** group related items together.
3. **Repetition:** reuse the same colours, fonts and shapes.
4. **Contrast:** make important things stand out.

### Margins

Keep important content **away from the edges**, with equal margins on all sides. Canva shows guides when objects line up.

### Images

- Use **high quality** images, and never stretch them.
- Match the **mood** of the brand.
- Use **free licensed** photos (Canva has many, or sites like Unsplash). Do not take images from Google without permission.

::: check Which images are safe to use?
- Any image from Google search
+ Free licensed images or ones you own
- Images from other designers
= Respect copyright.
:::

## 📱 Posts, carousels and stories

### Sizes

- **Instagram post:** 1080 × 1080 (square) or 1080 × 1350 (portrait, gets more space).
- **Story / Reel cover / WhatsApp Status:** 1080 × 1920.
- **Facebook / X link image:** 1200 × 675.

Canva has these sizes ready. Search "Instagram post" or "Story" when you create a design.

> 📌 **Remember:** design for the platform's size first, so nothing gets cropped.

::: check What size is a story?
+ 1080 × 1920
- 500 × 500
- 100 × 100
= Stories are tall, in a 9:16 shape.
:::

### Carousels that teach

A carousel is a post with **several slides**. A good structure:

1. **Slide 1:** a strong hook ("5 mistakes in your CV").
2. **Slides 2 to 6:** one idea per slide, short text.
3. **Last slide:** a call to action ("Save this and follow for more").

Keep the **same colours and fonts** on every slide.

### Keep text safe

On stories, keep important text **away from the top and bottom edges** where the app's buttons sit.

::: check What should you keep on every slide of a carousel?
+ The same colours and fonts
- A different style on each slide
- No text
= Consistency makes a series feel professional.
:::

## 🏷️ A simple logo and brand kit

### What makes a good logo

- **Simple:** works small, like on a phone.
- **Memorable:** a clear shape or wordmark.
- **Versatile:** looks good in colour, black and white, light and dark backgrounds.
- **Fits the business:** a bakery logo should not look like a bank.

> 📌 **Remember:** a good logo is simple enough to draw from memory.

::: check Which quality matters most in a logo?
+ It stays clear and simple, even when small
- It has as many details as possible
- It uses ten colours
= Simple logos are used everywhere.
:::

### Make one in Canva

1. Create a **Logo** design (500 × 500).
2. Start with the business **name** in a strong font.
3. Add a **simple icon** from Elements, or a letter in a shape.
4. Use **one or two colours**.
5. Download as **PNG with a transparent background** (this needs Canva Pro; otherwise use a plain white or solid background).

> ⚠️ **Watch out:** do not copy another brand's logo, and check that the icons are free to use.

### Brand kit

Write down: **main colour codes** (hex), **two fonts** and **logo versions**. Use the same ones on everything the business posts.

::: check What goes in a simple brand kit?
+ Colours, fonts and logo versions
- Only the logo
- Nothing
= A brand kit keeps everything consistent.
:::

## 📄 Flyers and printing

### The flyer formula

A good flyer answers three questions in seconds: **What is it? Why should I care? What do I do next?**

1. **Headline:** the offer or event.
2. **Image or graphic:** one strong visual.
3. **Key details:** date, time, place, price.
4. **Call to action:** how to order or contact.
5. **Contact info and logo.**

> 📌 **Remember:** a flyer is read in 3 seconds. Make the headline and the action obvious.

::: check What must a flyer make obvious?
+ What it is and what to do next
- Every detail about the business
- A long story
= People glance, so be clear.
:::

### Print or digital?

- **Digital** (WhatsApp, social media): export as **PNG or JPG**.
- **Print:** export as **PDF Print** at A5 or A4 and leave a small **margin** so text is not cut off.

### Check before you share

Read every word aloud. Check **phone numbers, dates and spellings**. Ask a friend to look at it for 5 seconds and tell you what they saw first.

::: check Which file type is best for printing?
+ PDF Print
- A screenshot
- A blurry photo
= PDF keeps quality for print shops.
:::

## 🧰 Design cheat sheet

### Before you export

1. **One message** is the biggest.
2. **Two or three colours**, two fonts at most.
3. **Contrast** is strong, and text is readable.
4. **Alignment and white space** are clean.
5. **Spelling and details** checked.

> 📌 **Remember:** simple, clear, consistent, readable.

### Sizes

- Post: 1080 × 1080 or 1080 × 1350
- Story: 1080 × 1920
- Flyer: A5 or A4 (PDF Print)

### Safe use

- Use free licensed images.
- Never copy logos or designs.
