## 👋 Welcome: the social media manager's job

Small businesses know they should be on social media, but they have no time. A **social media manager** plans, creates, schedules and tracks content, and talks with the audience, so the business grows.

- 📈 Analytics and reports

## 🎯 Goals, audience and platforms

### Start with the goal

Ask the client: **What do you want social media to do?**

- Get **awareness** (more people know about us)
- Build **trust** (show our work and reviews)
- Bring **leads and sales** (messages, orders, bookings)

> 📌 **Remember:** no goal, no strategy. A clear goal decides what you post.

::: check What should you ask first?
+ What the client wants social media to achieve
- Which filter is nicest
- How many hashtags exist
= Goals drive content decisions.
:::

### Know the audience

Write a quick profile: **Who are they? Where do they spend time? What problems do they have? What do they like to see?**

### Choose platforms

- **Instagram:** visuals, reels and stories. Great for food, fashion, beauty, events.
- **Facebook:** local communities, older audiences, groups, events and ads.
- **WhatsApp (Business and Status):** direct customer chats and quick updates.
- **X:** news, ideas and quick conversations.
- **LinkedIn:** professional services and B2B.
- **TikTok:** short video, reaching new people.

> 💡 **Tip:** do **two platforms well** instead of six badly.

::: check A small bakery wants to show cakes visually. Which platform fits best to start?
- LinkedIn
+ Instagram
- A newspaper
= Visual products work well on Instagram.
:::

## 🗓️ The content mix and planning

### A balanced mix

Do not only sell. A healthy mix is:

- **Educate** (tips, how-tos, FAQs): about 40%
- **Entertain** (behind the scenes, humour, trends): about 25%
- **Prove** (reviews, results, before and after): about 20%
- **Sell** (offers, new products): about 15%

> 📌 **Remember:** if every post says "buy now", people stop listening. Help first, sell second.

::: check Which mix keeps an audience interested?
- Only sales posts
+ A mix of useful, fun, proof and selling posts
- Only memes
= Variety builds trust and attention.
:::

### Content pillars

Choose **3 to 4 pillars**, themes you always return to. For a bakery: *Cake designs, Baking tips, Customer stories, Offers*.

### A weekly rhythm

| Day | Pillar | Format |
|---|---|---|
| Monday | Baking tip | Reel |
| Wednesday | Customer story | Photo |
| Friday | New offer | Carousel |

Three consistent posts a week beat a burst of ten and silence after.

::: check Why plan content pillars?
+ They make posting easier and keep the brand focused
- They stop you posting
- They are required by Instagram
= Pillars give you ideas to repeat.
:::

## ✍️ Captions and calls to action

### A caption that works

1. **Hook:** a strong first line (people see only the first line!).
2. **Value:** the useful or interesting part.
3. **Call to action (CTA):** tell people what to do next.

> **Example:**
> Stop buying cake before you read this 👇 3 questions to ask your baker: ... Which tip surprised you? Comment below, or message us to order.

> 📌 **Remember:** a post without a call to action wastes attention. Say what to do: comment, save, share, message, click.

::: check What is a call to action?
+ A clear instruction for what the reader should do next
- A hashtag
- A photo filter
= CTAs turn viewers into action.
:::

### Writing tips

- **Short sentences**, simple words and your brand's voice.
- Use **line breaks** so it is easy to read.
- Use **3 to 8 relevant hashtags**, not 30.
- **Proofread.** One typo can look careless.
- Add **alt text** to images for accessibility.

### Visuals

Clear, bright photos, consistent colours and fonts, and short **vertical video** perform well. A free tool like Canva helps (see our **Canva** course).

::: check Which caption opening is strongest?
- "Hi guys"
+ "3 mistakes that ruin your cake order 👇"
- "Post"
= A specific hook earns the next line.
:::

## 🗂️ Scheduling and tools

### Why schedule

Batch your work: **create once a week, schedule the rest**. It saves time and keeps you consistent.

### Free and simple tools

- **Meta Business Suite:** schedule Facebook and Instagram posts.
- **Google Sheets:** a content calendar.
- **Canva:** designs and a content planner.
- **Phone notes:** a bank of ideas.

### Your content calendar columns

*Date, Platform, Pillar, Format, Caption, Visual link, CTA, Status.*

> 📌 **Remember:** a content calendar turns "what do I post today?" into a plan you can follow.

::: check What does a content calendar do?
+ Plans what to post, where and when
- Posts for you without any content
- Counts followers
= It gives you a clear plan.
:::

### Best time to post

Check the page's **insights** to see when followers are online, then test and adjust. Avoid copying advice that does not fit your audience.

::: check How do you find the best time to post?
- Guess
+ Check insights and test
- Always midnight
= Use your own audience data.
:::

## 💬 Engagement and community

Social media is **social**. A brand that talks with people grows faster than one that only broadcasts.

### Reply fast and kindly

- Reply to comments and DMs **within a few hours** when you can.
- Use the person's **name**.
- Be **helpful and human**, not robotic.
- For questions about price or orders, move to **DM or WhatsApp**.

> 📌 **Remember:** speed and kindness build trust. A slow reply can lose a sale.

::: check Why reply quickly to comments and DMs?
+ It builds trust and can win sales
- It does not matter
- It confuses the algorithm only
= People expect a quick, friendly answer.
:::

### Handling a complaint

1. **Thank** them for telling you.
2. **Apologise** sincerely if the business was wrong.
3. **Offer a fix** or move the chat to private.
4. **Follow up** to confirm it is solved.

Never argue in public and never delete honest complaints.

::: check What is the best first step for a public complaint?
- Argue back
+ Thank them and offer to fix it, then move the chat to private
- Delete it
= Calm, helpful replies show others how you treat customers.
:::

### Build community

Ask questions, run polls, share customer stories (with permission), and reply to people who comment often.

## 📈 Analytics: what to measure

### The key numbers

- **Reach:** how many people saw your content.
- **Engagement:** likes, comments, shares and saves.
- **Followers growth:** new followers per week.
- **Clicks and messages:** the actions that lead to sales.

> 📌 **Remember:** vanity numbers (likes) matter less than actions that serve the goal (messages, orders, clicks).

::: check Which is closest to a real business result?
- Likes only
+ Messages and orders
- Number of hashtags
= Always connect numbers to the goal.
:::

### How to learn from data

1. Look at the **top 3 posts** of the month. What do they share (topic, format, hook)?
2. Look at the **weakest 3**. What went wrong?
3. **Do more of what works**, and less of what does not.
4. Test **one change at a time**.

### A simple monthly report

Include: **goal, key numbers, best post, lesson learned, next month's plan.** Keep it to one page.

::: check What should a monthly report include?
+ Key numbers, best post, lessons and the plan for next month
- A long diary
- Only screenshots
= Clients want results and next steps.
:::

## 🧰 Ethics, safety and your first client

### Be honest and safe

- Do not buy followers, fake reviews or copy others' content.
- Get **permission** before using customers' photos.
- Respect **copyright**: use your own images or free, licensed ones.
- Keep **passwords** safe. Use a **password manager** and two-step verification, and never share a client's login publicly.
- Follow platform rules and local laws on advertising.

> 📌 **Remember:** trust is the asset. Honest content and safe account handling protect the brand and you.

::: check Which is safe practice with a client's account?
+ Use two-step verification and keep logins private
- Share the password in a group
- Reuse your own password
= Account security is part of the job.
:::

### Finding your first client

1. **Practise on a real small business** you know, even for free, for a short, clear trial.
2. **Document results:** before and after screenshots with permission.
3. Offer a simple **package**: "12 posts + 4 stories + replies, per month".
4. Use the **Freelancing 101** course to write your proposal and price.

::: check How can you get your first proof of results?
- Wait for someone to find you
+ Run a short trial for a real small business and record results
- Copy other people's results
= Real results are your best portfolio.
:::
