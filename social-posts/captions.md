## Posting tips

- Post the square image on Instagram and Facebook, the wide one on X, and the tall one as a Story and WhatsApp status.
- Add a short video of the editor if you can: screen-record yourself typing in a lesson box and the preview changing. It usually gets more reach than a still image.
- Post at lunchtime or in the evening (around 12–2pm and 7–9pm) and reply to every comment in the first hour.
- Pin the post on your profile, and put the link in your bio.
